Stories people have trusted me to share

Survivor Stories.

When I asked people to share their stories, I didn’t know what would come back. People trusted me with experiences they’d carried for years. I’ve written about those stories here, and read some of them aloud for the podcast.

I’m grateful to everyone who let me share a part of their life. If something here feels familiar, I hope it helps to know someone else has been there, too.

If you’d like to reach out

Have something you want to share?

I welcome stories about religious trauma, medical trauma, domestic violence, childhood or family abuse, and other experiences of trauma and survival. Those are examples, not limits—you don’t have to fit a category or be finished healing to share your story.

You can contact me about your own experience, a story you read here, or a question you’ve been wanting to ask. You don’t have to tell everything to start a conversation. By submitting your story for Survivor Stories, you agree to anonymous publication on this website and/or narration on the podcast, with real names removed or replaced with pseudonyms. For a private message instead, use the regular email option on my contact page.
