# The Almanac’s daily readings

These twelve files mirror the revised Almanac of Stars: 366 dated readings, including February 29. The historical folder name is retained for existing links.

Use the interactive [Star Day guide](/astrology/) to find your birthday or today’s entry. The calendar is perpetual and symbolic, with fixed lunar stations; it does not track an astronomical moon. Readings offer images and questions for reflection, not guaranteed outcomes. Blood concerns unchosen forces or experiences, while Bone concerns available choices and responses.
