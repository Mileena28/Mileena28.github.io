About this book

She wanted a place to belong. He made sure she had nowhere else to go.

Five years after the Silence, refugees still arrive on Rivenglade’s shores with whatever they can carry. Into a town struggling to shelter them comes Lucien Altheris, The Son of Zenith. He offers something more precious than food or refuge: the promise that their suffering had a purpose.

Mira Roehart wants to believe him. Her best friend has found peace in his teachings. Her father trusts the future he promises. But Mira still loves the forest, the sea, and the old stories Lucien calls corruption. As his faith takes hold, the things that once connected her to the people she loves begin to divide them.

Then Lucien chooses her as his bride.

To Rivenglade, the marriage is a blessing. Behind closed doors, Mira learns what his tenderness conceals—and how little room there is to refuse a man everyone believes is holy. With each attempt to become the wife he demands, she feels herself slipping further away.

Yet something within her resists him. Something she cannot explain, bound to the forbidden woods and a stranger who knows a name only her father should have known.

Lucien has taught his followers to fear what waits beyond his Light. For Mira, it may be the only place left to turn.

Leaving will mean risking everything she knows. Staying may cost her everything she is.

The Chosen is a dark fantasy about coercive faith, survival, and the struggle to reclaim a self others have tried to erase.

Content note: Intended for mature readers. Contains religious abuse, coercive control, gaslighting, graphic violence, detailed depictions of intimate partner violence and sexual assault, and suicidal ideation.

About this book · Series spoilers

Pregnant and on the run, Mira finds sanctuary among the Forsaken—those cast out by Zenith's Light. But safety comes with its own price as she navigates resistance politics, forms unlikely alliances, and races toward the Verdant Veil while carrying a child whose mixed heritage could either restore balance or destroy it completely.

Book 3 · Upcoming

The Village

A new chapter of community, family, and healing in sanctuary.

About this book · Series spoilers

Deep in an ancient forest, Mira and young Kai (ages 3-8) find true sanctuary among nature spirits and Murmeleks. Here, far from Lucien's reach, Mira finally learns about her Nimwe heritage while mother and son build a harmonious life with the magical beings of the forest. This is a story of thriving rather than merely surviving, of healing old wounds and discovering what family truly means.

Book 4 · Upcoming

The Return

The final book brings the story toward a return home.

About this book · Series spoilers

Ten years after the events of The Village, Mira and eighteen-year-old Kai are done running. Armed with knowledge, power, and allies gathered over years of preparation, they return home to confront Lucien and the oppressive grip of Zenith's Light. This is the story of reclaiming what was stolen, protecting what was built, and proving that sometimes the greatest power comes from those who were once powerless.
