The story

The gods have grown quiet. Something else is answering.

Across Arcadia, creatures born of divine blood roam the lands scarred by an ancient war. Hunting them has given Kaelen a livelihood and a purpose. Then a voice begins speaking inside his mind, and the power stirring beneath his skin threatens to make him one of the things he has spent his life killing.

In the Verdant Veil, the forest no longer answers Elowen as it once did. Healers are disappearing. Violence is spreading among the people she has sworn to protect. Searching for its cause brings her into Kaelen’s path, where her determination to help meets a man with every reason to fear what help might uncover.

They are not the only ones listening. A prince resents the limits of his birth. A young cub flees a clan that intends to sacrifice him. An Inquisitor condemns those touched by divine blood while trusting that the gods approve of what he does to them. Each hears a voice that understands what they fear—and offers what they want most.

As fear turns neighbors against one another, the promise of power becomes harder to refuse. The answers may lie in histories their rulers and scholars have learned to dismiss, but seeking them will mean trusting people they have been taught to hate.

And whatever is speaking knows how much they want to be saved.

The Silence is a dark fantasy of divine inheritance, persecution, and the difficult bonds formed between people who have learned to survive alone.
