The story

Through Amina’s eyes.

Amina Korteka’s story unfolds on Nareen, an island with its own traditions and ways of seeing the world. When she falls in love with a boy from a distant, broken world, we experience that love through her eyes.

The Girl Beneath the Cherry Tree is a standalone companion to The Chosen Chronicles, bringing another perspective to the world of Blood of the Wild Gods.

How the story is told

A fairy tale inside the novel.

Every chapter opens with a passage of fairy tale. Read together, those passages tell the story in miniature.

The fairy tale draws on the oral tradition of a people who believe all things flow like water. It runs alongside Amina’s story, giving readers another way to experience it.
