The Five Foundations of Power

From raw cosmic essence to legendary artifacts that shape the fate of gods and mortals

Celestial Substances

Divine Essence

Light itself, harvested as material. The Weaver of Sorrows tells how the seer Malritha would wait until the full moon hung heavy, climb to the highest spire of her temple, and with a spindle carved from driftwood and fingers dipped in saltwater gather moonlight from the air like silver silk, winding it onto bobbins made from the vertebrae of deep-sea creatures. Where the light touched her fingers it sank into her skin and emerged again as thread—the raw light of heaven woven into something tangible.

Organic Components

Living Essence

Substances derived from supernatural creatures that retain something of their power long after death. The bones of a beached leviathan, built into a loom, pulsed with faint internal light as if the creature's spirit remained trapped within them, and trembled in recognition when their kin stirred in the deep. When a Nimwe departs she leaves behind an impossibly delicate silver feather that pulses faintly with otherworldly energy—proof and prophecy in one. These materials blur the line between object and entity.

Legendary Artifacts

Cosmic Relics

The Eclipse Pendant has a dark center, a narrow pale ring, and a chain. Mordain’s research attributes it to Zenith; that attribution is not independently confirmed. Recovered from the Awakening Tomb, it warms and hums when Kaelen wears it, then amplifies his divine inheritance during the final battle. After Elowen brings him back, it gives no response to the Lorekeepers’ examination; whether it is exhausted or altered remains unknown. The Crown of Aetheris is distinct from Solara’s ordinary royal crown. It amplifies Verites within Dain’s mortal body and shatters after Dain’s death. In The Weaver of Sorrows, Malritha’s Leviathan Loom weaves moonlight into patterns of destiny. Novel references: The Silence, Chapters 11, 27, 37–38, and 42–44.

Sacred Tools & Implements

Ritual Instruments

Specialized instruments for channeling dangerous magical forces. The Free People keep bundles of dried herbs—some for their healing properties, others for more arcane uses, plants known to open the mind and blur the veil between waking and vision—which they add to the fire for the seeing smoke. Silver worn as blessed plate stands as a barrier against the taint of the Beastborn, and the High Inquisitor of the Hollow Spire channelled his life-draining magic through a skull-carved staff of Beastborn bone.

Relics of the Fallen Gods

Fragments of the Divine War

The battlefields of the Divine War are strewn with what the gods left behind, and whole cultures have grown up around the salvage. The nomadic Ashborn of the Godscarred Wastes seek power and protection not in temples but in shards of divine armor, petrified tears of goddesses, and splinters of god-weapons; their children dream of unearthing god-stones and wielding shard-blades. Hunters forge armor from the hides of Wasteland Crawlers and reinforce it with silver plate scavenged from the dead. Greater relics gather origin stories as well as power: Solarian tradition links Aetheris to Solarius, and Mordain’s research attributes the Eclipse Pendant to Zenith. Their observed effects do not independently prove those accounts of their makers.
