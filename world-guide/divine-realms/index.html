Divine Realms & Cosmology

"Where gods govern reality and cosmic forces shape the very fabric of existence"

Journey beyond the mortal realm to explore the celestial hierarchy that governs all existence. From the crystalline Hall of Eternal Judgment to the primordial angels of the elements, from the First Gods who spoke reality into being to the Wild Gods who loved mortals into catastrophe, discover the pantheon, the cosmic politics, and the terrible mercy of divine silence that determine the fate of gods and mortals alike. Here, consciousness shapes reality at scales that dwarf mortal comprehension.

Divine Realms

Sacred domains where divine beings dwell and cosmic power flows unconstrained

Hall of Eternal Judgment

Cosmic Government Seat

The crystalline amphitheater serving as the primary governmental structure for the organized pantheon. Divine thrones arranged in ascending tiers catch and refract light that has no earthly source, painful for mortal eyes to perceive, and the seating reflects cosmic hierarchy—the First Gods highest, the younger gods below. Reality itself becomes negotiable within the hall: temperature, gravity, and the flow of time respond to the collective mood of the assembled deities, and divine conflicts can make the foundations of heaven tremble. It has not echoed with divine footsteps for centuries, but can be summoned when cosmic-level decisions require collective divine input.

Trace the Divine History

Celestial Gardens

Senia's Domain of Creation

Senia's domain exists in perpetual golden light where reality unfolds into impossible beauty. Flowers bloom in fractals of pure radiance, each petal containing entire worlds; trees of crystallized sunshine sway in breezes that carry the music of distant stars. The Workshop of First Light holds the forges where concepts—justice, beauty, hope—are given physical form, and the Sanctuary of Growing Things serves as nursery for every soul before it is born into the material plane. The realm brightens with its ruler's joy and dims with her sorrow.

Meet Senia

Twilight Boundary

Neutral Divine Meeting Ground

The liminal space where day surrenders to night, serving as neutral ground for divine meetings. Neither sun nor moon rules here completely, allowing opposing cosmic forces to interact without diminishing each other's essential nature. The Parliament of Shadows convenes here when divine law requires formal deliberation, and the Neutral Repositories—vaults outside normal time and space, opened only by unanimous divine consent—hold the First Words that began creation, weapons capable of killing gods, and the names of concepts too dangerous to speak.

Meet Elodias, God of the Moon

Oceanic Underworld

Ancient Leviathan Depths

The domain beneath the Azure Expanse where ancient leviathans dream in trenches that predate current creation. The Dreaming Depths house consciousness older than the gods—leviathans that experience all possible versions of reality at once, their dreams the source from which prophets draw visions—while the Bone Metropolis rises from the skeletons of ascended leviathans, former sea gods who chose to become infrastructure rather than fight for cosmic dominance.

Sail the Azure Expanse

River of First Light

Primordial Waters of Creation

The primordial location where humanity was shaped from responsive clay. This cosmic waterway ran with a color that had no name—somewhere between dawn and dream, between hope and memory—its banks soft with dust fine as ground pearls that had never known footsteps. Here the boundary between potential and actualization was thinnest, and consciousness could be breathed into waiting matter. The River still flows through hidden channels connecting all waters everywhere: every spring from an unknown source, every well that never runs dry.

Meet Thainos, Who Shaped the Clay

The Deep Places

Wild Gods' Exile Sanctuaries

Locations where the surviving Wild Gods have retreated to maintain their self-imposed isolation—Echo Caves that resonate with unspoken words, Untrodden Forests where divine presence can be felt but never encountered, Living Tombs where gods have buried themselves while remaining conscious and aware. They speak only in rare sacred spaces built to contain the force of divine words, and even there their voices must be muffled lest reality distort. Only the most extraordinary circumstances might draw a Wild God from its tomb.

Remember the Great Withdrawal

The Pantheon

The gods whose loves, judgments, and silences shaped Arcadia—each with a fuller account among the Characters

The First Gods

Those who awakened in the void before creation and spoke reality into being: Leika, Mother of Mountains, who raised the mountains from her breath and taught stone to reach skyward; Libros the Lawgiver, living mathematics, who inscribed the laws that hold reality in place; Archadeus, who gave the hunt its hunger and the predator its purpose; Atonia, who distilled truth from chaos into beauty that cuts like winter wind; and Verites, who from the spaces between light and substance whispered darkness into being—not as absence but as presence, as necessary as breath. Libros's angels, Archadeus's beastmen, and Atonia's elves are their handiwork. They view mortals as mayflies, and it was they who set their brother Thainos his thousand-year test.

Meet the Mother of Mountains

Thainos, the God Who Wept

The god who shaped humanity from responsive clay on the banks of the River of First Light, giving them softer skin than made sense, hearts that could break from mere words, and souls that could contain infinities. Challenged by his siblings to walk among his creations stripped of immortality, he searched a thousand years for one human untouched by cruelty. The Wanderer constellation marks the moment his search ended and his essence scattered like seeds across all humanity—the Scattered Divinity every mortal now carries.

Meet Thainos

Elodias and Senia

Elodias, god of the moon, of tides and quiet stillness, and Senia, goddess of the sun, of blossoming life and radiant beauty—bound by a curse woven from moonlight, shadow and blood by the jealous seer Malritha. Senia reincarnates endlessly as a mortal with no memory; Elodias retains perfect divine recall; and when he finds her she has seven days after remembering before her mortal form dissolves. The Lovers constellation formed the instant he chose eternal cycles of love and loss over never seeing her again. Senia's domain is the Celestial Gardens.

Read Seven Days of Sun

Azrael, the Fallen Guardian

God of Guardian Angels, who commanded the protective hierarchy that stood between divine predators and mortal prey—until the day he chose mercy over divine law, killed his corrupted brother, and was cast from heaven. Walking the earth bearing the weight of necessary sin, he found in a small forest village that redemption comes not from divine forgiveness but from the stubborn love of those who see past the monster to the man: Rynn, and the impossible child Hope.

Meet Azrael

Silas, the Trickster

The Trickster God, of oil-slick eyes and silver hair, shifting form and claws disguised as nails, who operates outside all factions. It was Silas who offered divine blood to a grieving mortal named Astrid Ravenscroft and so became the architect of the Bloodmarked—the gift that taught mortals divinity could die, and the first thread of the war that ended the age of open divine presence.

Meet the Trickster

Khali, the Dream Shepherd

The ancient Dream Shepherd, who chose to walk among mortals as a simple healer in a cottage where the river knew her name though she had never told it. When she caught the eye of both a foreign prince, Eshuah, and a jealous princess, Jade, divine love became the catalyst for mortal tragedy—and gold rewrote what followed into a mural of a radiant Vaelari striking down a shadowed figure glowing with ethereal darkness. Her true story is told in The Song of Blood and Stone.

Meet Khali

Zenith and Solarius

Zenith, God of Gods, and Solarius, the Sun God—the most prominent of the Wild Gods, the faction that believed mortals should walk among divinity without being preyed upon. The golden kingdom of Solara was built where Solarius fell, and its Crown of Aetheris holds the Sun God's last divine spark. The elves of Sommer Isle keep what they call the purest worship of Zenith, and in the years after the Silence a new faith proclaimed him the one true God and every other deity a demon. Of Zenith's own relics, the best known is the Eclipse Pendant.

Walk the Ruins of Solara

The Silent God

A consciousness older than memory, dormant since before the dawn of the divine war itself: a deity whose name was erased from all but the most sacred texts, whose temples crumbled to dust, whose existence became a whispered heresy. Not a god of thunder or fire, earth or water, but of the void itself—the silence before creation, the stillness after destruction. Its awakening came not with a roar but with whispers, and its purpose was not dominion or worship but the unmaking of existence. Its stolen vessel met its end at a hunter's blade in the ruins of Solara, yet the Awakener constellation burns eternal as a warning.

Hear the Whispers

Divine Hierarchy & Politics

The cosmic governmental systems that determine the fate of gods and mortals alike

The First Gods

Ancient beings who established the fundamental laws of reality and claim ultimate authority over cosmic governance. They view mortals as mayflies—brief creatures whose individual lives matter less than maintaining cosmic order and divine supremacy. Their apparent cruelty, the historian Tanneus G'Raja came to believe, masks a desperate restraint learned through catastrophe.

Study the Ancients

The Wild Gods

The faction of deities who believed mortals deserved active divine protection, walking among them as friends, protectors, and guides, and hearing every desperate plea. Their love, expressed without the restraint that keeps the infinite from overwhelming the finite, began cascading consequences—divine words reshaping existence, blessings becoming curses—that ended in the Divine War, reality unraveling, and their withdrawal to the deep places. Their whispers still reach the Dawnseekers of the Verdant Veil, they bestow blessings upon anyone, even those who do not follow their will, and in the Hollow Spire's forbidden texts their vibrant creation stands opposed to the Silent God's stillness. To Zenith's Chosen they are false idols and demons.

Remember the Fallen

Cosmic Government

The crystalline governmental structure where divine beings present cases, make accusations, and render judgments that reshape existence's fundamental laws. It operates as both courtroom and parliament: the First Gods occupy the highest tiers, while younger gods—courtiers who arose after creation's first phase, often crueler and less wise than their elders—are relegated to lower positions. Reality itself becomes negotiable within these cosmic chambers of authority.

Meet the Lawgiver

Divine Political Tensions

Ongoing conflicts between divine conservatives who believe in maintaining the status quo and progressive factions arguing for different approaches to divine-mortal relations. Three factions endure: the Orthodox Hierarchy, which enforces strict separation between realms; the Progressive Underground, which seeks safer forms of contact and ways to heal past damage; and the Isolationist Wild Gods, who have chosen complete withdrawal and silent witness. Trickster gods thrive in the cracks between them, and these tensions can erupt into cosmic conflicts that reshape reality.

Witness the Conflicts

Guardian Angel System

The protective hierarchy established under Azrael's command to prevent divine exploitation of mortals while maintaining beneficial contact between realms—monitoring divine-mortal interaction zones, verifying that mortal free will remained intact, and limiting displays of power that could traumatize mortals. This system successfully regulated divine-mortal interactions for millennia before its tragic end.

Honor the Guardians

The Elemental Angels

Angels govern each of the six elements—Earth, Air, Fire, Water, Spirit, and Void—as the primordial forces of creation and balance. They can be invoked only through a deep understanding of the ancient texts, the celestial alignments, and the language of the angelic realms. The Angel of Light, opposite of the Void, appears clothed in white garments of celestial fire, with void-black eyes and burning red pupils and a sword like a diamond blade, and grants its aid only in exchange for the supplicant's complete erasure from existence.

Study the Six Elements

The Silence as Mercy

The central mystery of mortal theology—why don't the gods answer?—has an answer the historian Tanneus G'Raja called poison. The gods are not absent, deaf, or indifferent: they hold themselves back with a restraint so absolute it looks like abandonment, because they learned through apocalypse that divine words reshape existence, that every blessing becomes a curse in time, and that love expressed directly from the infinite to the finite does not elevate but incinerates. Their cruelty was kindness; their distance was love. “Pray they never love us enough to speak again.”

Gods of Other Shores

Divinities from works still in development—premise-level only, as the author has published them; names, domains and details may change

The Dragon (The Song of the Dragon — planned trilogy)

The most ancient and mysterious of the constellations appeared when the First Gods discovered something that predated them—something so terrible they built it a prison among the stars. What lies imprisoned is lost to memory, but since its inception all who grieve have heard the same song in their sorrow, and within its notes some claim to hear promises of paradise unlocked, of death undone, of reunion with all that was lost. The Song of the Dragon tells how, in a distant age, the scholar Eren Glavier loses his beloved Raewyn to plague, follows a map hidden in the song's verses, and breaks the seal imprisoning The Dragon—an entity with the power to resurrect anything that has ever died. Raewyn breathes again, but The Dragon's freedom begins resurrecting the Old Gods who hunger for vengeance.

Read the Dragon's Prophecy

The Three Gods of Hevetica (The Senach — in development)

Across the sea lies the Imperial Republic of Hevetica, a nation of towering, long-lived Bohdkin whose three gods created them and never left them. Ordinary Bohdkin are blessed—their weapons carry god-granted magic and their nation runs on divine favor—but only the seers, the state's oracle class, are infused with divine magic through a ritual of devotion, and only to the seers do the gods speak. Where Arcadian divinity is ancestry carried in the blood, Bohdkin divinity is borrowed rather than born. Certain their order is superior, they call an Arcadian a Senach—godless savage. The names and domains of the three gods, and where they stand in the ancient schism between First Gods and Wild Gods, are questions the author has not yet answered.
