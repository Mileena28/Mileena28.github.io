Southern Trade Corridors

"Where commerce flows freely and neutral ground enables prosperity"

Networks of cities, roads, and waystations that facilitate commerce between the major kingdoms, representing humanity's attempt to create neutral ground where political differences matter less than profitable exchange. Here, the accumulated trust of generations ensures that trade flows regardless of the conflicts between larger powers.

The Heart of Commerce

The Southern Trade Corridors are the networks along which goods, services, and information flow across kingdom boundaries through carefully maintained networks of mutual benefit. These routes have been developed over generations, creating infrastructure that transcends political loyalties. Business along them is conducted in the Common Tongue—Arcadian, the trade language of most human settlements, with its regional dialects, its formal registers for legal proceedings, and its habit of avoiding the direct names of dangerous supernatural things.

Success here depends not on royal favor or divine blessing, but on reputation, reliability, and the ability to honor contracts regardless of changing circumstances. The corridors operate on commercial law that prioritizes profitable exchange over political allegiance. Yet commerce in Arcadia must always account for the supernatural: merchants keep emergency reserves against periods of divine crisis, maintain alternative routes that avoid areas prone to divine activity, favor flexible currencies that hold their value through supernatural disruptions, and price the risk of supernatural threats into every venture.

From exotic spices to magical components, from rare books to refined metals, everything finds its way through these bustling networks—including the complex markets for rare materials with supernatural properties, service guilds offering specialized supernatural labor, protection contracts insuring against supernatural threats, and brokers dealing in forbidden knowledge and dangerous intelligence. Much of what is recorded of them comes from the compendium compiled from the chronicles of the scholar Tanneus G'Raja.

The Free Cities

Independent settlements operating under commercial charters that prioritize trade law over royal decree

Commercial Independence

Each Free City maintains its own currency, legal system, and defensive forces while participating in the broader trade network. Their prosperity depends on maintaining strict neutrality in conflicts between larger powers, prioritizing commercial relationships over political allegiances. This independence allows them to serve as neutral ground where even enemies can conduct business.

Commercial Infrastructure

The physical and institutional foundations that enable prosperity to flow across kingdom boundaries

The Great Road

A Thousand Miles of Trusted Passage

Stretching for over a thousand miles, the Great Road is paved with stones quarried from seven different kingdoms and maintained by the Merchant Guilds Coalition. The road's magic lies not in supernatural power but in the accumulated trust of generations of traders who've honored its neutrality.

River Markets

Floating Commerce Adapts to Politics

Massive barges that can be repositioned based on seasonal demand and political conditions, floating on rivers and coastal waters. When tensions rise between kingdoms, the markets simply float to international waters where no single authority can impose its will. This mobility ensures that trade continues regardless of political upheavals on land.
