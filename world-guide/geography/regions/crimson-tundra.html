Crimson Tundra

"Where strength is forged in ice and tempered in blood"

A frozen wasteland in the far north where perpetual winter reigns, inhabited by the fierce Bloodfang Clans whose lives are devoted to strength, survival, and the worship of the War God. Under the pale gaze of a weak winter sun the land stretches out in a monochrome tapestry of white and grey, broken only by jagged peaks of black ice and the crimson stains of ancient battles frozen into the landscape—the blood-stained snow that gives the tundra its name. Blizzards howl across the endless plains, the air bites with teeth of frost, and life clings to the very edges of survival. This is a land where only the strong survive, and weakness means death.

The Endless Frost

The Crimson Tundra is a realm sculpted by brutality and etched in frost, a vast, desolate expanse perpetually locked in winter. The geography is harsh and unforgiving—endless plains of frozen snow broken by jagged peaks and towering cliffs of black ice, frozen rivers that serve as highways for those who know their secrets, and ravines where those rivers carve through the ice. The land itself is said to be tainted by the blood of the War God, and the crimson stains of ancient battles lie frozen into the snow.

Weather patterns defy prediction—blizzards transform familiar terrain into trackless white death, and the wind howls like a banshee across the plains, biting through even the thickest furs. At the tundra's biting edge, a rare tavern such as The Weary Wanderer offers travelers a pocket of hearth-warmth against the cold, while those coming up from the south provision themselves with sturdy packs, thick furs and supplies in the forest town of Oakhaven before braving the frozen road north.

Hidden between the cliffs lie valleys sheltered from the wind and wrapped in an unnatural stillness that hums with unspoken power. There, half-buried in the drifts, crumbling stone structures emerge like the skeletal remains of colossal beasts—broken walls, shattered pillars, fragments of colossal statues—the remnants of an ancient god-battlefield where gods clashed, titans fell, and the earth was soaked in the residue of divine war. Residual power still thrums in the frozen ground, and beneath one such battleground lies the Awakening Tomb.

The Bloodfang Clans

Berserker tribes of Beastmen—intelligent, bipedal creatures mirroring the wild forms of the tundra's fauna—organized around strength-based hierarchies and devotion to the War God

Pack Hierarchies

Bloodfang society is rigidly hierarchical, ruled by strength and savagery and bound by bloody tradition. The clans are nomadic warriors, following the migrations of tundra beasts in a cycle of hunting, raiding and survival. Strength is not merely valued—it is worshipped as the sole measure of worth; honor means dominance, measured in violence and displays of ruthless power. Compassion is viewed as weakness and mercy as a vulnerability to be exploited, so that life among the clans becomes a winnowing in which only the strongest, the most ruthless and the most cunning survive. Titles such as Alpha Female mark rank within an encampment.

The Sacred Hunt

Bloodfang society revolves around hunting not just for survival but as a form of religious expression. Cubs prove themselves in rite-of-passage hunts—to bring down a massive Ice Boar single-handed, as young Ryx Shadowclaw once did, is a feat unheard of for one so young.

Blood Moon Ceremonies

Complex ceremonies centered around the Blood Moon, when the moon casts its crimson glow across the snow, the veil between worlds thins, and divine power flows most freely. On such nights the encampment is engulfed in ritual frenzy—burning herbs scent the air, war drums pulse through the frozen ground, and the elders' chanting rises in fervent supplication to the War God. Their religious practices involve consuming the blood of the Bloodmarked upon frozen altars, in rituals believed to amplify strength, sharpen senses, and forge a stronger bond with the War God.

Clan Diversity

The Bloodfang Clans are an alliance of Beastmen species, and each species keeps its own tribe within the clan while all are bound by Bloodfang law and their common veneration of the War God. Hulking Ursine Beastmen form the backbone of the warbands, their brute strength unmatched in close combat; swift, cunning Lupine Beastmen run as scouts and raiders, their senses honed by generations of chasing prey across the frozen plains; winged, piercing-eyed Avian Beastmen patrol the skies as sentinels and messengers; and lithe Feline Beastmen serve as hunters and assassins, moving like liquid shadow across the snow. Families cross these lines—the feline cub Ryx Shadowclaw was born to Korgath, a hulking Ursine of considerable standing, and Lyra, his black-furred mother, whose movements were as fluid and silent as falling snow.

The Awakening Tomb

Trial of Gods

Hidden beneath an ancient god-battleground, behind a snow-choked fissure and a spiral of descending stairs, this mystical trial site forces entrants to confront their deepest fears through elaborate illusions. Whispers of fallen Wild Gods, trapped within the battleground's stone and raw with primal grief, warn that one's deepest, darkest fear is faced here; then the tomb's architecture shifts and changes, separating groups and creating individualized trial chambers. Those who pass demonstrate they have overcome their greatest psychological barriers, proving their worthiness for sacred artifacts and knowledge. It was here, in The Silence, that the Eclipse Pendant of Zenith lay hidden, and here that Kaelen, Elowen, Ryx and Mordain faced the Trial of Gods. Read more about the Awakening Tomb.

Bloodmarked Sacrifice

Ultimate Offering to the War God

The most sacred and terrible of clan rituals, and a cornerstone of clan life. Any child born with unusually potent Bloodmarked abilities is held to be not merely blessed but sacred—a living conduit to the War God whose divine power can be harvested through sacrifice. When the elders read the signs, the family is lauded, envied and elevated, for to offer such a child is the pinnacle of devotion; the child is washed in numbing snowmelt, adorned with ritual bone markings, and offered upon a frozen altar under the glare of the Blood Moon, their lifeblood spilled and consumed by the clan's warriors. Whether the offering wills it or not is of no account—it is a cycle of power and fear that feeds itself. In The Silence, one mother's defiance of this tradition, and the price she paid for it, drove her cub Ryx into exile and set him on the road south.

Ice Boars

Tundra Game

Massive beasts of the frozen plains, among the hardy tundra animals whose migrations the nomadic clans follow. To bring one down single-handed is a feat unheard of for a cub—the kill by which young Ryx Shadowclaw revealed his Bloodmarked nature during his rite-of-passage hunt.
