About Mileena Rayne

Hi, I’m Mileena.

I’m a writer, musician, registered nurse, and mom of two girls from Minnesota. I’ve spent a lot of my life getting lost in other people’s worlds. Now I have one of my own, and I’m a little obsessed with it. This website is where I share my books, music, thoughts, and the things I’m still figuring out.

Who I Am

I didn’t really start writing and making music until I was almost thirty. I’d wanted to write a book since I was a kid, but for a long time I was just trying to get through whatever was happening in my life. Actually finishing one felt a little unreal. Now The Silence and The Chosen are sitting on my shelf, and I get to keep going.

There’s a lot of my life in what I make. I grew up as a Jehovah’s Witness, left at eighteen, and have spent years working through things I didn’t always have the words for. Writing gives me a way to look at some of that. It also gives me characters to care about, an entire world to get distracted by, and something I’m excited to share.

A Little More About Me

I feel things deeply, and I’m usually thinking about more than I probably need to. Sometimes that means overthinking a conversation. Sometimes it means spending hours on a detail in my fantasy world because I want it to make sense. I get excited about things, and I’m trying to get better at letting myself enjoy them without worrying so much about what someone else will think.

I also care a lot about how people are treated. That matters to me as a nurse, as a mother, and when someone trusts me with their story. I know what it’s like to have something hurt and struggle to explain why. I want to listen before deciding I understand.

Music is another part of that. Sometimes I’m writing about something personal, and sometimes I’m making music for the world inside my books. I like being able to explore the same feeling in a different way and hear what a story might sound like.

Leaving the Jehovah’s Witnesses

I grew up going to Kingdom Halls, knocking on doors, and believing we had “the truth.” At eighteen, I left. That meant questioning things I’d been taught my whole life and knowing it would affect relationships with people I loved. I didn’t leave with everything figured out. I had to start figuring it out afterward.

“I didn’t leave with everything figured out. I had to start figuring it out afterward.”

That experience shows up in my writing, especially The Chosen. I’m interested in what happens when people are taught to distrust themselves, when love feels conditional, and when something harmful has been called normal for so long that it’s hard to recognize. Putting those experiences into a story gives me a different way to talk about them.

There’s more to my history than leaving a religion. Childhood, relationships, health, and trying to keep life together have all played a part. Sometimes those things are hard to separate. I don’t have a neat explanation for how to get through all of it, and I don’t want this page to sound like I do.

For a long time, I wanted nothing to do with anything the organization had touched, especially the Bible. More recently, I’ve been finding some comfort in scriptures I grew up with. I’m not returning to the organization. I’m giving myself room to decide what I believe and what helps me, without someone else deciding that for me.

Speaking Up in My Community

In December 2025, I spoke with Valley News Live after my former fiancé was released on $500 bail following an assault against me. I wanted people to understand what that decision meant for my safety. The interview drew attention in my local community and opened up conversations about low bail in domestic violence cases and the need for reform.

The Worlds I Grew Up In

As a kid, I looked for the thickest books I could find. More pages meant more time with the characters and less time having to come back to everything else. Games did that for me too. These are some of the worlds I spent the most time in, and I can still see pieces of what I loved about them in the things I create.

Books That Raised Me

Eragon Series+

I loved getting attached to the characters and having such a big world to spend time in. Knowing Christopher Paolini started young mattered to me too. It made the idea of writing my own book feel a little more possible.

Inkheart Series+

The idea of being pulled into a story was pretty much everything I wanted from a book. I wanted to stay there long enough that the characters felt like people I knew.

Kira-Kira+

This was the first book that made me cry. I still loved it, even though it hurt. I think that stayed with me: an ending doesn’t have to be happy for a book to mean something to you.

Chronicles of Narnia+

I loved how much there was to imagine, and how attached I became to the world and everyone in it. That feeling of wanting to go back to a place that doesn’t actually exist is something I understand very well.

Games I Lived In

EverQuest Online Adventures: Frontiers+

My first online fantasy world. I spent so much of my childhood in Tunaria, and I still remember the stories from those questlines. There were times when being there felt safer than being in my actual life.

Final Fantasy Online+

This is where Rayne came from. I used that name in the game, spent years in the community, and it became part of how I knew myself. Eventually I kept it. Online friendships and the places we make them can mean a lot more than people give them credit for.

Elder Scrolls Online & Skyrim+

I was the dual-wielding warrior picking up every quest, wandering into caves, and trying to learn every crafting skill. I loved finding out that some little detail connected to something much bigger. I still think about that when I’m working on my own world and deciding when a reader should learn something.

Ark: Survival Evolved+

I liked being able to build something in a place where so much could go wrong. There’s something satisfying about making a home in a game that is constantly trying to kill you.

I spent years caring about characters and places someone else had made up. Now I get to make my own, and I hope they’ll mean something to someone else too.

My Journey

Childhood

Lost in Other Worlds

My dad introduced me to fantasy games, and I spent a lot of time reading. I wanted the longest books and as much time in those worlds as I could get.

Age 18

Breaking Free

I left the Jehovah’s Witnesses. I had to question what I’d grown up believing and figure out what life looked like outside it.

Nearly 30

Finding My Voice

I started writing and making music. After wanting to write a book for so long, I was finally doing it.

2025

Starting Over

My daughters and I spent two months in a hotel and then a month in a shelter. Our room there was underground, without windows. The shelter gave us somewhere to stay and people who could help. We got housed again. I still sleep with the blankets they gave me.

November 2025

Speaking About What Happened

After the assault and my former fiancé’s release on $500 bail, I spoke with Valley News Live. The interview led to a much bigger local conversation about bail reform and survivor safety.

2026

Holding My Books

I got to hold The Silence and The Chosen as actual hardcover books. I’d kept the promise I made my fourth-grade teacher. My first book signing happened in my car outside a UPS store, wearing a Bob Marley shirt from the shelter. That’s how it happened, and I like that I get to remember it that way.

Present

Where I Am Now

I’m still on leave from nursing and planning to return to the Resource Team at the end of October. In the meantime, I’m taking care of myself, spending time on my writing and music, and trying to give myself room to enjoy my life too.

Current Focus

Healing on Purpose

I’m still on leave from nursing, with plans to return to the Resource Team at the end of October. I spent eight and a half months in the operating room, but the schedule was hard to make work with the rest of my life. Right now I’m taking time to work through what’s happened and take care of things I kept putting off. I want to go back feeling more ready, rather than just pushing myself through another day.

Sharing Other People’s Stories

Sharing other survivors’ stories matters to me. People trust me with things that aren’t easy to talk about, and I want to take the time to get them right. I hope someone reading them recognizes a feeling or an experience they thought they were alone in.

Building My Fantasy World

Right now, I’m doing a major rewrite of The Silence. It’s the first book I finished, and I want to bring everything I’ve learned since then back to it: better scenes, more grounded characters, and the lore I developed as the world grew. The new cover is already out, and the rewritten book will be re-released soon. The Forsaken manuscript is complete, and I’m also working on The Girl Beneath the Cherry Tree and the wider Blood of the Wild Gods universe.

What I’m Hoping For

I would love to make a life out of writing. I’m grateful for the people who buy a book, listen to a song, share something I’ve made, or take the time to tell me what they thought. It means a lot to know someone is spending time with a world that started in my head. I still dream of being able to just write and write and write.
