If you’ve been looking for a sign not to give up, this is it.

I remember when I first started posting publicly, I heard what people were saying about me behind my back. It hurt. But I was on a mission, and I couldn’t just stop because some people didn’t like what I had to say.

At first, I posted out of anger. My relationship of almost three years had ended in violence, and my entire world had been turned upside down. I wanted to advocate for harsher criminal penalties in domestic violence cases and bail reform. I wanted people to know what had happened to me, and I wanted something to change because of it.

Then I started posting because I was sad. I felt completely lost in life and didn’t really know who to turn to anymore. Sometimes it was easier to talk to strangers on the internet than to explain what was going on inside my head to someone I knew.

Eventually, I realized there were a lot of people who could relate to what I was going through. And if sharing my experiences could make even one person feel less alone in their own struggles, then it was worth doing.

And I still post for that reason.

But somewhere along the way, I picked up a little side mission.

I wanted to see if I could use the algorithm to find the perfect person for me.

I figured if I just kept posting whatever came into my head, speaking my mind, sharing my sense of humor, my interests, and just being myself, maybe eventually the algorithm would put me in front of someone who genuinely liked the person I was.

And you know what?

It actually worked.

One of my posts somehow ended up on his TikTok For You page. And he found me. Someone who turned out to be everything I’d been hoping to find.

And to think that all of this happened because of a sad post I made on the internet.

If I had listened to the people talking about me behind my back, the people who made me feel stupid or embarrassed for putting so much of myself out there, I might have stopped a long time ago. And if I had stopped, that one post might never have made it onto his phone.

If you’ve been looking for a sign, this is it.

Don’t give up.
