My ex strangled me hard enough to leave bruises on my neck. He was arrested, and then walked out on a $500 bail—even though they knew he needed a mental health evaluation and I told them in my police report that he was planning to leave the country.
He has a prior assault for strangling another woman—a felony pled down to a misdemeanor. He has an active warrant in Colorado for fleeing that state. They had all of this information. So why was his bail basically the cost of a Costco run?
I’ll tell you where he is now: Mexico City.

That’s his fresh start. Meanwhile, I’m standing in the wreckage—healing, rebuilding, trying to move forward—while the system that was supposed to protect me decided that a man who strangles women was worth $500 and a wave goodbye.
This is what “justice” looks like. Why are we letting violent people walk out of jail so easily?
