There’s magic in the world.

Not the kind of magic people think about on television, but the real magic that lives in the energy between all living things.

During a time in my life when I studied esoteric beliefs, there was a daily practice I learned, and I want to teach it to you now.

Every day, no matter where you are, no matter how you’re feeling, no matter what battles you might be fighting, find one thing where you are and find the beauty in it. Just one thing. Maybe it’s the way the light falls across the room. Maybe it’s the sound of wind moving through the trees. Maybe it’s the color of the sky, or the way someone smiled at you, or the simple fact that something soft and beautiful still exists in the middle of a hard day.

Be still in the beauty. Stay with it for a moment. Let yourself fully see it. Let yourself feel what it is doing to you. And then feel grateful for the beauty you found in this world, and grateful that it exists at all.

Do this every day, and it will change your life. Not because it will erase your pain or make the world easier, but because it will slowly teach you how to remain open to something sacred inside of it. It will remind you that even in the middle of struggle, there is still beauty here. There is still energy. There is still magic in the world.
