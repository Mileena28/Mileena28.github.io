Thank you to all of my dear friends I’ve made and come to know.

You have been a light in my darkest nights. I almost wasn’t here anymore—my heart was so heavy, so hopeless, so alone. And one by one, you gave me back the strength to keep going. Every message of kindness. Of concern. Of wisdom. Of connection. And slowly, I feel like you’ve helped make me whole again.

I’m so lucky. I’m so grateful. I’m so blessed.

I wish I could name every one of you, but please know your compassion has made a difference in my life. And for that, I am eternally grateful.
