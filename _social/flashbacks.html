I wonder when I’ll stop getting flashbacks.

It’s usually when I’m putting my key in the lock to the front door.

It’s like my whole self is transported back to a life that, in a way, now feels like a bad dream.

Don’t be too tired to listen to him talk.
Don’t wear the wrong look on my face.
Don’t make him angry.

Don’t give him a reason to start a fight with me.

And then I remember he’s gone.

I turn the key. Walk up the stairs. Open the door and walk down the same hallway he pushed me down. Where he put his hands around my throat. Where he threatened to kill me.

A ghost of a moment that still haunts me.

But now it’s quiet.
And I can have whatever expression feels right on my face.
And there’s nothing waiting for me but cats that missed me while I was gone.
