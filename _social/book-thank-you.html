I’m so grateful for every person who has taken a chance on my writing. Thank you for believing in me—for stepping into a world I built out of everything I’ve survived.

Every time someone shares that they’re reading my work, it means more to me than I can really put into words. I don’t think that feeling will ever become normal, and I don’t want it to. I never want to reach a point where I take that kind of support for granted.

I want to keep celebrating these moments, keep sharing them, because you deserve to be seen and appreciated just as much as you’ve made me feel seen.

And maybe one day, if I’m lucky, this could become more than my hobby. Maybe it becomes the life I get to live every day.
