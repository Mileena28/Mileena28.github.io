Tomorrow will mark one year since it all happened.

One whole year.

I gave you everything. Literally everything. My body, my loyalty, my money, my protection. I gave you everything I had to give.

And what did I get in return?

You called me evil. You called me stupid. You called me a bitch. You told me everything was my fault.

I didn’t deserve what you put me through. I didn’t deserve your abuse. And while there’s still a part of me that’s angry, a part of me that hates you for everything you did, there’s something else I want to say.

Thank you.

Thank you for finally crossing that line in the sand. For pushing me to a point where I knew I had to leave, and for giving me every reason never to look back.

Because now, when I come home from a 12-hour night shift, I get to come home to peace. I don’t have to worry about being yelled at, having a fight started over nothing, or losing what little sleep I have because you decided I didn’t deserve to rest.

Because now, there’s a gentle, kind man in my life who has shown me what it feels like to be understood. To be loved for who I am, without having to beg for kindness or constantly defend myself.

He doesn’t hurt me. He doesn’t swear at me or talk down to me. He lifts me up. He encourages me. We’re building a future together where we both get to succeed, where we support each other instead of tearing each other down.

And it’s made me realize just how much I was willing to tolerate because I thought loving someone meant never giving up on them.

I know you still find ways to read my posts. I know you’ll probably see this.

And part of me wishes you could understand what I’m saying. That you could recognize how much your words and actions hurt the people who care about you.

But that’s something you have to figure out for yourself.

You blame everyone else for the relationships you’ve destroyed. You cut people off, convince yourself you’re the one who’s been wronged, and even when you claim to look inward, you somehow never see the part you played in any of it.

And that’s why you’re alone.

That’s why you’ll keep losing people who genuinely care about you. Because you can’t fix something you refuse to acknowledge, and you can’t keep hurting people and expect them to stay.

One whole year.

Thank you for lighting the match and burning down the future I thought we were going to have together. Because if you hadn’t, I might never have walked away long enough to discover the life I have now.

And I wouldn’t trade it for anything.

Have a nice life.
