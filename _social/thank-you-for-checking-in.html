Thank you to everyone who has checked in on me. I’m sorry I’ve been so quiet lately.

I’ve been spending a lot of time working on the soundtrack for The Silence, and honestly, sometimes it feels good to disappear into my world and live there for a little while.

It means more than you know to be missed. I don’t always realize that my presence can make someone’s day a little better, so hearing that has been really touching. Thank you for reminding me that I matter.
