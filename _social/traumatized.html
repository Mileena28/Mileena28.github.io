I think I honestly feel a little traumatized right now.

Today at work was a good day overall. It went well. I saw a lot, and I learned a lot.

But I also saw some really fucked up shit.

The kind of shit that stays in your head. The kind of shit that feels like it belongs in a horror movie, not in real life right in front of you. There are some things I really don’t think most people should ever have to see, because once you do, they haunt you like a ghost.

It was an honor to be part of what I was part of today, and I truly mean that. But it was also extremely dark. Extremely heavy. And right now, I’m just so grateful to be alive.
