I left the Midwest, left the roads I’d driven every day
Scared to leave, but scared that I would never get away
Fifteen hundred miles from everything I’d ever known
Somewhere no one knew my name, somewhere I wasn’t alone

The air was crisp, the trees were different, flowers everywhere
You watched the road; I watched the world and wished I could stay there
The same face with a different future, room to start again
I hadn’t even left you yet; I missed you even then

How can I miss a place
I had only just met?
How could a life I haven’t lived
Be one I can’t forget?
