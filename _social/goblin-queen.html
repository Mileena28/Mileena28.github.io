Does anyone remember when I said I lived like a goblin?

I wonder how many of you have been here with me long enough to remember that version of me.

Lately, I feel like I’ve gone back to being that person again. Because honestly, it’s just easier to not care so much anymore.

To just let things go.

I used to hold myself to such a high standard. I wanted to do everything right, do things by the book, do things the way you’re supposed to do them because that was important to me.

And it still is.

I miss being that person. I miss having the energy to care about all those little things. I miss being able to hold myself to those standards and actually meet them.

And I really hope I can be that person again someday. But right now, it feels so good to just not freaking care.

Don’t get me wrong, I still try. I still do the best I can. But my best now is what my worst used to be.

And I don’t really know how to feel about that.

Because on one hand, it’s almost a relief to not have all those expectations hanging over me anymore.

But at the same time, I miss who I used to be.

I miss the version of me who could keep up with everything. Who had the energy to care about doing things right, even when nobody else would notice.

I hope I get back to her someday. I really do.

But right now, I’m so tired.

I’m just so tired.
