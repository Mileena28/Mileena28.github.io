Tonight was the first time I’ve ever been trick-or-treating for Halloween!

I wasn’t allowed to celebrate Halloween growing up because I was raised as a Jehovah’s Witness. So it was actually really fun getting to experience something I missed out on as a kid. Going from house to house, seeing all the decorations, and just enjoying the holiday.

Celebrating holidays is still a little strange to me, and I’m slowly getting used to doing things I never got to experience growing up.

I couldn’t decide on a costume, though, so I just dressed up as myself. 🙃
