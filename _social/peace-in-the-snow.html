Yeah, it sucked shoveling all that snow by myself today. But do you know what didn’t suck? Coming home in peace. Not walking through the door after a 12-hour shift to an angry man ready to yell at me, call me a stupid bitch, and make me feel like no matter how much I did, no matter how much weight I carried for both of us, it was never enough. I bent myself in half for a long time trying to keep the peace, trying to be enough for someone who had already decided I never would be. So yeah… I guess I really can’t complain too much.
