I can hear the falling rain
As it taps against my windowpane.
The quiet gives me too much time
To sit here with my shame.

I want to go somewhere far from here,
Where nobody knows my name.
The same face with a different future,
And a chance to start again.

I want to sing a new melody,
To find my voice in a brand-new song.
I can’t keep living in the place
Where everything went wrong.

Take me away, take me away.
