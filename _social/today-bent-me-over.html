Today bent me over and spanked me hard.

I’m just kidding 😆… but NOT entirely!!

I genuinely had a physically hectic and emotionally turbulent day, and I’m just now finally getting the chance to relax and read comments for a while.

I keep being blown away by what you all write to me.

It still doesn’t feel real. Part of me is still waiting for someone to jump out and say, “Haha! It’s all a joke. Nobody actually cares. No one ever did.”

Because I’m not used to this.

I’m not used to so many people saying such nice things to me. I’m not used to people believing in me. I’m not used to being noticed and understood. I was the weird, creepy emo girl growing up with like three friends, so to have this many people showing up with kindness, encouragement, and genuine care feels very strange for me.

But I hope you all know you’re doing a really kind thing for a random sad girl on the internet.

And she really appreciates everything you bring to her life.

And it makes her smile every day. 🙂

(And thank you to everyone that complimented me on my hair today)
