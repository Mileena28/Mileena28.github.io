Why I make music

My Musical Philosophy

I love creating. I love having an idea in my head and experimenting until I find a way to bring it to life. Music gives me another way to tell stories, explore feelings, and spend time in the worlds I’m imagining.

I enjoy using different tools and trying things I haven’t tried before. Sometimes that means bringing folk instruments together with electronic beats. Sometimes it means taking the same story and hearing how different it feels with another melody, another rhythm, or no lyrics at all.

I like exploring where genres meet and what I can make from that. I don’t want every song to sound the same. I want room to be curious, to keep learning, and to create the sounds I hear in my head.
